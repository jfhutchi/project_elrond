# Implementation decisions and tradeoffs

Implementation source d56ff4dcfcdb55bd060aa8898d65c1ff97df89c0 is accepted by the integration owner after the independent whole-branch review and scoped repairs. These decisions preserve the approved scope and tradeoffs; they grant no operational authority.

The implementation follows the user's ten-session clarification: consider learning daily after ten completed exchange sessions, train on all eligible retained history, and give the latest ten sessions raw weight 2 versus 1 for older sessions. Quiet sessions count. Validation uses only earlier information; protected, incompatible, unpriced-cost, or temporarily dirty outcomes are excluded until eligible. Insufficient evidence yields a recorded NO_CHANGE.

The core has three independent roles: entry trading, owned-position protection, and outcome processing with bounded learning. Ordinary provider or clock acquisition failures cause scoped waits followed by fresh-evidence retries. Manual pauses and genuine accounting/ownership contradictions remain explicit. This preserves useful PAPER experience without using a persistent global stop for ordinary transient faults.

Original client IDs, fills, plans, ownership and historical evidence survive restart and maintenance. Unknown costs stay unknown. The initial COLLECT model is nonpredictive; synthetic learning evidence has no operational broker authority. Existing legacy holdings keep their original protection and are not adopted or liquidated by installation.

Four final review repairs were accepted: bounded changed/pending historical work with durable correction notifications; explicit code-only runtime transitions that preserve economic identity; audited entry resume; and atomic commissioning plus initial activation. New schema36 tables are projections and authority records, not permission to rewrite old payloads. Arbitrary economic-policy or cross-owner migration remains outside this change.

Comparator payload duplication is a documented nonblocking P3, without a measured timeout claim. Actual provider latency/quota capacity, selected-token operation and a natural broker-PAPER loop remain operational acceptance work. Local tests and synthetic trades do not prove those outcomes.
