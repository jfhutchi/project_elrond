## Implementation

Added `PaperFundsEvidence` and one central `entry_inventory_objections` validator in the small `funds.py` module. `BROKER_PAPER_BALANCE_V1` applies only to `SIGNAL_OR_EXPLORE_V1` + `BROKER_PAPER` + `PAPER_TIME_V2`. Synthetic and old MODEL_ONLY callers retain exact cash requirements. `ReconciliationProof.funds_authority` is omitted when absent; old serialized proofs and frozen data were not rewritten.

After exact runtime-adapter admission and ordinary event recovery, guardian synchronization retains two account snapshots, two position snapshots and two complete open-order reads, local capture start/end and bounded monotonic duration. It persists the typed immutable acquisition in existing `core_provider_acquisitions` and binds its content ID to the proof in the certification transaction. No new schema or provider abstraction. Failed entry-only order acquisition does not itself prevent quantity reconciliation. Entry authority requires empty order coverage; owned protection can proceed with unavailable entry funds.

The central validator checks the persisted proof digest/equality, correct full-or-quantity certificate and latest generation, baseline ID, exact account/release/lane, current quantities/fill/order membership, current ledger-state digest, unchanged cash-completeness facts and known cash equality, revocation, exact receipt digest/content/commission/store/config/source, stable snapshots, five-second capture, 120-second local receipt age, restrictions and positive funds/equity. It never compares another process's monotonic origin. This helper is consumed by claim, the actual coordinator gate, compatible successors (including same-proof), locked final send, operational health recovery and explicit entry resume. Pure evaluation accepts only a scoped structural marker; it cannot grant submission authority.

The actual coordinator additionally reads all current open orders for the new policy and rechecks current account, quantity/basis and risk bounds. Compatible successors preserve original and current source bindings and cash equality. The unresolved-intent check now precedes the same-proof shortcut, so an unrelated unknown local submission cannot be bypassed.

No changes to ledger accounting, fee/net P&L fields, costs, V3 target, schema migrations, provider cash-activity semantics, frozen exports or `independent_cash_available`. Unknown fees still produce `cash_complete=False`, `expected_cash=None`, genuine quantity certificates and no ordinary RECONCILED/full-cash certificate for those proofs.
