Unknown allocated fees do not imply permanent entry prohibition. Without full cash authority, only `core_quantity_certifications` receives a fill-derived quantity/basis proof. Raw observed account cash is retained but is not a full cash certification. Prior full snapshots are explicitly revoked; retained certified-only readers, new claims and the actual final-send guard cannot use them to authorize entry. Owned reducing exits continue with attributable quantities and fresh broker position checks. Cash mismatch after complete authority is refused. Conflicting source replay rolls back business rows, preserves the rejected envelope in a durable scoped incident, quarantines affected outcomes, and refuses core entries without mutating the manual/global stop.

## Limits and rollback

Only the explicit synthetic adapter supplies commissioned complete activity/cash evidence here. This is not a claim Alpaca currently supplies complete fee allocation or per-fill cash movements; empty fee activity cannot prove zero. Trade bust/correction authority beyond fee revisions is refused pending a reviewed correction contract. Unsigned/unproven legacy evidence cannot grant ownership. Prepared unsent entries remain reserved unless the existing definitive-unsent mechanism records refusal; unknown POSTs are never retried. Future role launch/scheduling, clock policy, learning and deployment remain later milestones.

Rollback for this uncommissioned work is a source revert in the isolated branch while retaining evidence/fixture backups. A deployed v31 store requires compatible readers or forward repair. Never restore pre-trade state over newer broker events, erase revisions, clear a pause, or downgrade away evidence.
