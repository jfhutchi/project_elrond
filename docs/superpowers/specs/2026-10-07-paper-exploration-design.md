# Outcome-first PAPER exploration

The operator's October 7 instruction authorizes PAPER exploration before a favorable
prediction exists. This supersedes forecast-edge entry selection only for this
separately identified exploration policy. It authorizes no live route, credentials,
holdout reuse, larger aggregate risk, or fabricated statistical promotion.

## Deliverable

Reuse the real broker, reconciler, risk sizing, independent stops/deadlines and
durable ledger. Collect protected exploratory entries and their actual attributable
wins/losses, then record immutable feedback updates and evaluate subsequent
predictions on later unseen observations. Keep baseline forecasts as diagnostics;
their score cannot veto exploration. Missing data and safety vetoes still can.

## Policy and selection

Use the already verified six sector ETFs and genuine complete five-minute cohorts.
Freeze deterministic round-robin selection independently of forecasts and observed
profits. Select only an unheld instrument with no unresolved owned order or lot.
Retain one concurrent new learner position, not one trade per day. After a terminal
reconciled exit, a later fresh cohort may create another entry. No quota forces an
order. Initial position value is capped at 0.5% of equity, below the existing 5%
ceiling; retain the 0.25% entry-risk ceiling, 1% stop, 30-minute horizon and all
tighter cash, exposure, drawdown, calendar, clock and emergency-stop controls.
Inherited positions retain their original exit owner and count toward account risk.

Use a policy artifact and exact code/config binding, not an invented fitted model
or a fabricated high forecast. Transfer entry ownership explicitly from DIA;
retain observation, protection and independent exits. Record authentic operator
instruction provenance and actual operational deployment. Do not mark the policy
profitable, qualified or live-eligible merely because it can collect PAPER data.

## Evidence, costs and learning

Register the exploratory selection hypothesis before execution. Store provider
hashes, feature and availability times, policy/decision identity, baseline model
forecasts, each risk veto, intent/acknowledgement, actual partial fills, lot ownership,
protection handoff, exit reason, reconciliation, fee completeness and residuals.
Retain ten-basis-point assumed round-trip cost for a separately labeled conservative
comparison; retain actual fees and reference-versus-fill slippage without double
counting. Unknown costs or ownership make an outcome incomplete, never zero profit.

Each newly completed, reconciled episode creates an idempotent immutable feedback
receipt containing wins/losses/flat counts, net results, errors against the recorded
baselines and explicitly labeled error hypotheses. These summaries are descriptive
updates, not claims of a fitted or improved predictive model. Successor fitting
remains subject to the existing charged six-call aggregate budget, protected data,
chronological splits and sufficient-session gates. Later unseen evaluation remains
separate; do not silently overwrite model versions or automatically promote one.

## Alert repair

Keep strict selected-entry heartbeat verification. Only a separately verified,
fresh PAPER clock/calendar proving closure may classify an old selected receipt
as an expected wait in the research worker's operator status. This never grants
entry health/authority. Preserve alerts for unknown/open sessions, clock problems,
invalid selection/binding/consent and other real selected-worker failures.

## Acceptance and rollout

Test negative/zero baseline scores yielding an exploratory candidate, and ordinary
forecaster scores retaining their cost veto. Test PAPER/live isolation, hard vetoes,
partial-fill ownership, no duplicate entry on restart, independent exits, missing
fees/data, immutable outcome updates and chronological later evaluation. Use
synthetic broker tests only as code evidence. Final rollout backs up nonsecret
configuration/task definitions and consistent state; preserve subsequent evidence
on rollback. Do not interrupt protection to switch entries. Actual acceptance needs
a real PAPER entry, protected exit, reconciled net outcome and feedback receipt;
passing tests or a submitted intent alone does not establish this loop.
