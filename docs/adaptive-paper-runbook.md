# Bounded adaptive PAPER research

This workflow is experimental. Passing code tests is not a deployed trade, a
forward sample, or evidence of profitability. The existing frozen entry model,
independent deadline supervisor and protection services retain their own pins.

## Commission in stages

1. Finish the exact-head frozen dependency, lint, Linux/Windows typing and test
   contract in `scripts/ci_checks.py`, then the independent whole-candidate review.
   Hosted checks and branch protection must be reported accurately. Unknown
   protection is not authority to bypass it.
2. Qualify six to ten actual instruments using official asset identity and real
   data coverage receipts before freezing the manifest. Do not invent ETF
   constituent/company mappings. Register the hypothesis, raw-return target,
   chronological split, cost/risk policy, constant baseline and ridge recipe.
   Canonical guards must permit the actual archive intervals before reading data.
3. Create a pinned `ExperimentLaunchConfig` with `mode=SHADOW`,
   `trading_enabled=false`, `maximum_workers=2`, a separate experiment database,
   the existing configured environment-file **path**, authoritative operational
   and declaration database paths, and the actual verified clock receipt.
   The first collection-only stage has `research_sources=null`.
4. Run `deploy/omen/adaptive-paper-rollout.ps1` without `-Apply`. It checks clean
   source/configuration/manifest pins, clock health, the existing Limited
   Interactive learner principal and a new task name. The task is precisely
   `Elrond Adaptive Paper Shadow`; no core task is replaced. The Python preflight
   refuses fitting-enabled configurations at this stage.
5. Apply only the reviewed stage. Back up validated config/manifest and any
   existing experiment store consistently, verify SQLite integrity, and export
   task definitions. Credential contents are never copied. There is no
   operational migration, operational database write or protection interruption
   for this collection-only stage. Preserve all original services and pins.
6. Verify the real scheduled receipt, bounded worker start/completion, genuine
   provider/calendar coverage, the following natural tick, and the actual
   read-only HTML/API. A completed closed-session tick is liveness, not a trade.
   Configure the independent monitor with the exact experiment source pin only
   after validating its local measurements. New experiment alert codes remain
   local until their exact predefined catalog and existing chat route are
   reviewed; no unknown code or freeform exception text may transit GitHub.
7. Separately commission guarded immutable historical archives and charged
   research. The 20 training / 5 seen-validation sessions are exploratory input;
   historical retrieval times stay actual. There are at most six permanent
   charged calls including failed, timed out and unknown attempts. Unknown
   started requests are not retried. Fit children have a 15-minute limit.
8. Present the exact initial artifact/package, code/data/config hashes, roster,
   risk limits, limitations and rollback model for authenticated action-time
   PAPER activation. Broad consent, a fitted candidate, a package or a successor
   does not grant execution. Do not auto-promote or switch active models. A later
   active switch requires a separately reviewed flat/ownership-safe procedure;
   the initial activation helper deliberately rejects implicit switching.
9. Transfer exclusive entry ownership only with the exact approved binding,
   healthy clock/calendar, retained promotion and risk gates, no unresolved
   outbox/lot, and fresh independent protection/deadline evidence. Shadow
   launcher code cannot construct an order route. No live route exists here.

## Retained controls and evidence

- At most one **new** position, no more than 5% position value, 0.25% entry risk,
  a 1% stop and a 30-minute horizon; retain every tighter existing control and
  the fixed minimum 10 bps round-trip cost floor. Existing positions remain
  grandfathered under their original exit owner. Never liquidate/reset/adopt
  them to manufacture capacity or experiment profit.
- Entry dispatch occurs only inside the retained `RunOnceCycle`, after kill,
  reconciliation, data freshness, clock, measured risk and independent deadline
  checks. A claimed uncertain send uses same-client-ID lookup/reconciliation;
  it never blindly retries. Model selection cannot override any veto.
- Market labels/forecast errors, reference simulation, fee-bearing owned broker
  lots and daily account equity are distinct. Unknown fees, ownership,
  protection, terminal quantities or flat reconciliation leave owned economics
  incomplete. Synthetic outcomes never become real experiment P&L.
- The existing daily-close reader is the only account-calendar source. Cash
  flows/resets and missing closes are not profit or invented zero days. The
  profitable-day denominator includes all valid completed observed days,
  including flat days. Account equity includes legacy and unrealized marks;
  their unverified separate contribution must not be called learner profit.
- Successor scheduling adds five fresh training days and reserves five later
  unseen validation days. Prior validation dates stay excluded permanently.
  Fixed chronology chooses rows/parent; returns never select lucky variants.
  No successor receives inherited activation or changes the constant baseline.
- Twenty valid account days allow an account assessment, not a strategy-profit
  claim. Correlated symbols and overlapping windows are not independent sample
  counts. At 30 verified completed scheduled sessions, stop new experiment fits
  and entries; continue protection and evidence reconciliation. Missing calendar
  coverage blocks new fits/entries instead of inventing weekdays. Five coverage-valid completed research sessions
  without a measured entry declaration require development review.
- News is observational and point-in-time. Publication, actual first seen,
  retrieval, scope and coverage remain explicit. Fund news is not constituent
  filings. Missing SEC contact/access is a setup blocker, not guessed identity
  or permission to transmit the user's address. No paid provider is enabled.

## Recovery and rollback

`deploy/omen/adaptive-paper-rollback.ps1` defaults to plan-only. It requires the
exact commit marker, Limited Interactive principal and matching exported action
before stopping/unregistering only the new shadow task. It never restores an
old operational database, deletes experiment evidence, resets trial charges,
disables risk controls, stops original protection or changes power/security.
Retain new fills and all audit receipts. Preserve a failed rollout's backup
directory for investigation; do not reuse/overwrite it.

A clock failure is an engineering/clock-source problem, not another AI model
choice. Honor canceled elevation. Calendar waiting, missing source/adapters,
insufficient statistics and exact artifact approval are separate states with
explicit owners. Actual component failure remains visible even after close.

## Prospective acceptance owner

The implementer and parent retain the next naturally eligible session through
genuine forecast, eligible intent, fill, independent protected exit, verified
fees/flat reconciliation, owned outcome, fresh registered successor and later
authorized active decision. A veto is reported as a veto; no order, outcome or
profit is fabricated to complete an issue. The 70-80% profitable-day target is
aspirational and never enables live trading automatically.

## Observational provider journal connection

The optional `position_research_database` and `position_research_commit` must be
configured together. The coordinator opens that existing separate research
journal query-only, verifies each content-addressed signal against its run and
pinned producer version, and imports only exact roster identities. It does not
configure a new provider, infer ETF constituents or send a SEC contact address.
A missing/uncovered/failing producer remains UNAVAILABLE; a receipt older than
ten minutes is STALE. Original publication, retrieval and source-journal
availability are retained. News arriving after the feature decision time cannot
inform that decision, even if the experiment journal is written later.
Each scored decision gets a thesis record with observational authority only.
The existing strategy's forecast, hard risk and exit authority remain unchanged.

Crash recovery reads at most two unresolved prior target sessions on each wake,
rotating by least recent auditable attempt. Missing old targets cannot starve
later dates. Recovery runs before current observation collection; failed old
queries leave current acquisition running and retain sanitized failure receipts.
Expired current features remain observations but cannot create decisions or
orders; the next boundary is a calendar wait, with all freshness limits retained. Canonical date protection covers the actual UTC price-query bounds
before retrieval or archive writes. Recovered labels become available at actual
retrieval, never at the historical target timestamp. The shared canonical trial
transaction serializes the six-fit budget across independent experiment roots;
a previously reserved request cannot invoke another engine after local-store
loss. A terminal broker acknowledgement can finish a claimed experiment journal
only when its identity and terminal state are verified, without resubmission.
