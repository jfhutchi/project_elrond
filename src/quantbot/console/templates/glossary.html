What is deliberately not here

Advice. This page says what the words mean, not what you should do about them. Whether a threshold is set correctly is a question about evidence, and this system does not yet have enough forward evidence to answer it — a glossary that implied otherwise would be the most useful-looking wrong thing on the site.
