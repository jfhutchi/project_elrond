Discovery

What the anomaly detector and catalyst enrichment have produced. Both subsystems have failure modes that look like success from a total, so the outcome mix is shown rather than a count: a run of negative findings reads as a stream of discoveries, and a run of unknowns reads as nothing happening at all.

Unresolved incidents

Contained failures. Anomaly detection, regime snapshots and catalyst enrichment all describe decisions rather than making them, so a failure in any of them costs a row of research context and never a trading cycle. They are recorded here so the resulting gap in the dataset is visible rather than silent — a later study can tell “no anomaly fired” from “detection was broken that day”.
